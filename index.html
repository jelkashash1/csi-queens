THE MURDER OF MARCUS RIVERA

Marcus Rivera, a 34-year-old property manager in Astoria, Queens, was found dead inside his apartment on Tuesday evening.

Investigators believe he was killed between 8:30 PM and 9:30 PM.

Earlier that day, Marcus told a friend he had discovered suspicious financial activity involving someone he knew. He planned to reveal the information the following morning.

Your Mission

Investigate the crime scene, examine evidence, question suspects, and identify the person responsible for Marcus Rivera's death.

Meet the Suspects

👔 Daniel Brooks

Marcus's business partner. They recently argued about money.

🏠 Sofia Martinez

Marcus's neighbor. She claims she heard an argument that evening.

📦 Kevin Thompson

Marcus's former employee. He was recently fired.

👩 Rachel Rivera

Marcus's sister. They recently disagreed about family finances.

Investigation Rules

  • Do not jump to conclusions.
  • Document every discovery.
  • Distinguish facts from assumptions.
  • Use evidence to support your final accusation.

DAY 2 · FORENSIC ANALYSIS

New Evidence Has Arrived!

Forensic investigators have reviewed the evidence. Four additional discoveries may help solve the case.

Evidence E – Shoe Impression

The shoe impression found near the entrance is consistent with a shoe belonging to Daniel Brooks. His right shoe has a damaged heel matching the missing section documented in the impression.

Evidence F – Security Camera

A nearby building camera records a person entering at 8:38 PM and leaving at 9:12 PM. The person wears a dark business jacket. Their face is not visible.

Evidence G – Financial Records

Investigators confirm that Daniel transferred company money into an account he controlled. Marcus had discovered the transfers and planned to report them.

Evidence H – Phone Records

The unknown number that sent Marcus the threatening message is traced to Daniel Brooks.

Suspect Interrogations

👔 Daniel Brooks

"I was at a restaurant that evening. Marcus and I had business disagreements, but I never went to his apartment. I had nothing to do with his death."

He produces a restaurant receipt showing a purchase at 8:12 PM.

"I was home that evening. I heard two people arguing around 8:45 PM. I couldn't identify their voices, but one person sounded very angry."

📦 Kevin Thompson

"I was upset when Marcus fired me, but I was working a delivery shift that evening. My supervisor can confirm I was there."

👩 Rachel Rivera

"Marcus and I argued about money last week, but we made up. I was visiting my parents that evening. I had no reason to hurt my brother."

The Killer: Daniel Brooks

Motive: Daniel was stealing money from the business. Marcus discovered the transfers and planned to expose him.

Evidence Supporting the Solution

  1. Financial records connect Daniel to unauthorized transfers.
  2. Phone records connect Daniel to the threatening message.
  3. The shoe impression is consistent with Daniel's damaged shoe.
  4. Security footage establishes an opportunity to be at the building.
  5. Daniel claimed he never visited the apartment, which conflicts with the combined evidence.

Teaching note: A shoe impression consistent with a shoe is not unique identification. A restaurant receipt at 8:12 PM does not prove an all-evening alibi. Students should consider all evidence together.
